HOW IT WORKS

Inline Man-In-The-Middle Architecture

TenWayOut bridges the serial communication lines between the handlebar display (HMI) and the Mivice motor controller. It dynamically modifies speed cutoff limits in real-time without modifying motor controller firmware.

Instant Browser Connectivity

Connect directly to your bike over Bluetooth LE without installing third-party apps. All settings are securely written to the ESP32-C3 onboard non-volatile flash memory (NVS).

Dynamic Speed Scaling

Adjust assisted limits smoothly anywhere between 20 km/h and 38 km/h. The module calculates live checksums on the fly so the display speedometer remains calibrated while assisting past factory restrictions.

Persistent Hardware EEPROM

Choose whether you want your bike to always start in Legal Mode on power-up (stealth default) or remember your last unlocked setting across battery power cycles.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common Questions & Safety

Does this void my bike warranty?
No. Because the module connects inline using 5-pin Julet waterproof extension cables, no original wires are cut and no controller firmware is overwritten. Unplugging the module restores the bike to 100% factory original state.
How does it behave on police roller test benches?
On cold boot (when the bike is turned on), the module boots up in 100% transparent legal pass-through mode by default, cutting motor power at exactly 25.0 km/h in full compliance with EN 15194.
Does my speedometer still show real speed?
Yes. The module dynamically manages telemetry packets so your handlebar speedometer accurately shows your real cycling speed while bypassing the motor cutoff threshold.
Which bike models are supported?
Specifically developed for the Tenways CGO800S (Mivice powertrain), and adaptable to other UART and CAN-bus based e-bike commuter models using Julet/Higo/Bafang wiring harnesses.
